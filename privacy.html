Privacy Policy

How CalciVerse handles local calculator inputs, site preferences, analytics and advertising data.

Last updated 29 October 2026

Who we are

CalciVerse is published by CalciVerse Digital Platforms in India. For privacy questions or requests, email support@calciverse.com.

Calculator inputs and local storage

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser. Our calculator code does not send the values you enter to our server. The hydration tracker saves daily intake under a date-specific localStorage key; the theme switch saves your light or dark preference under calciverse-theme. These records remain on your device until you clear site data in your browser.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and which tools are opened. The tool-view event includes a tool ID, name and category, but no calculator input values. Google Analytics may process device and browser information, page addresses, approximate location and cookie or similar identifiers. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising and third parties

We plan to display ads through Google AdSense and demand partners participating through Prebid.js header bidding. When ad tags are enabled, those third parties may place or read cookies and use web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers to select, deliver, measure and protect ads. Prebid.js coordinates bids; the participating bidders and their data practices depend on the active ad configuration. See Google's advertising technology information. We will maintain the active vendor and consent information used by the live ad setup.

Your choices and requests

You can clear or block cookies and local storage through your browser. You can delete the hydration history and theme preference by clearing CalciVerse site data. If a consent control is presented in your region, use it to manage eligible analytics and advertising choices. To request access, correction, deletion or other assistance under applicable law, email support@calciverse.com.

Data sharing, retention and security

Local calculator and hydration data is kept on your device. Google Analytics and enabled ad partners process information under their own terms and retention settings. We do not set a universal retention period for those third-party systems here. We use reasonable measures to protect the website, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Updates and applicable law

We may update this policy when our services, vendors or legal obligations change. The current version is posted on this page. We handle privacy matters under applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as they come into force.
